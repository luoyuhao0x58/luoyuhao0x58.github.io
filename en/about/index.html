About this site

The reason I made this site is to have a place of my own — not an account that can disappear at any time, but a place where I decide the layout, the categories, and how things are preserved for the long run. Many ideas used to scatter across notes, chat logs, and bookmarks, always meant to be “organized later” and mostly never were. Now I want to put what’s worth keeping carefully into a place that will stay accessible for a long time.

The other reason is that I’m a programmer myself. Rather than only talking about architecture and engineering in articles, I’d rather actually maintain a small system — domain, server, database, cache, search, comments, RSS, backups, monitoring, SEO. These trivial but real problems push me to turn “knowing” into “doing”. This site is my long-term project.

“Write code, and write the world beyond code”. As I get older, I increasingly feel there is much more worth caring about beyond technology. So besides tech, there will be games, films, music, and records of hobbies that may not form a system but are real.
